Our kitchen

Atrium began with a simple idea: a restaurant should feel like a favourite room in a friend's home, only with better fire.

Chef Mara Vale built the kitchen around produce, not fashion. We work closely with small farms and fishers, make almost everything in-house, and let the best ingredient on the day take the lead.
