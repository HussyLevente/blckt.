The garage, decoded

Blackline began as a one-bay operation behind a machine shop. The principle has stayed stubbornly simple: a fast car deserves a slow, exact look at what makes it fast. Today, our shop pairs old-school mechanical feel with instrumentation precise enough to catch what intuition misses.

We document the work because trust should be inspectable.
